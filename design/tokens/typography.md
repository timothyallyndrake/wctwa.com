# Typography tokens

Working direction, grilled 2026-10-14. Load from Google Fonts. Fall back to a system sans.

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Line height | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `type-display` | Barlow Semi Condensed | 700 | `clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 4.75rem)` | 1.02 | Page titles and defining statements. Tracking `-0.02em` |
| `type-heading` | Barlow Semi Condensed | 600 | `clamp(1.75rem, 2.4vw, 2.25rem)` | 1.15 | Section and program headings |
| `type-body` | Atkinson Hyperlegible | 400 | `1.125rem` | 1.6 | Descriptions, reading copy, form content |
| `type-caption` | Atkinson Hyperlegible | 700 | `0.875rem` | 1.4 | Labels, metadata, schedule details |

Families to load: Barlow Semi Condensed 600 and 700; Atkinson Hyperlegible 400 and 700.

Display size is part of this scale so the welcome statement can carry the page. Body size stays comfortable on a phone.
